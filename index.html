Project 01

Mereb Multi-Vendor
Marketplace

A full-scale e-commerce ecosystem connecting local Ethiopian merchants with customers. Merchants register and list products; customers browse, cart, and pay. Vehicle owners register as delivery partners and earn income on each fulfilled order — creating a distributed logistics network from scratch.

Features: multi-vendor commission system, Stripe & Cash-on-Delivery, distance-based delivery fee calculation, wholesale pricing, coupon engine, and real-time order tracking codes for pickup & delivery.
